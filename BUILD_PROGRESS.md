## Important operating limits

- The current Upstox option-chain adapter timestamps quotes at API response receipt. Upstox does not provide a documented per-contract exchange bid/ask timestamp through that endpoint. - Paper fills are immediate at displayed bid/ask with no latency, partial fills, transaction costs, expiry settlement, or live broker orders. Do not use paper results as expected live P&L.
- The worker skips exits when market data is unavailable or unsafe. A real account would need feed quality, market-session, expiry, escalation, and settlement controls before unattended trading.
- The historical replay now supports one manual position or a bounded fixed-expiry schedule, not repeated signals or an intraday strategy backtest.
