# NIFTY paper-data source decision — 21 October 2026

**Decision: retain Upstox as the configured observation provider; do not add DhanHQ execution or buy its Data API now.** Neither provider's reviewed public documentation proves a distinct exchange-origin update time for the executable bid/ask on each NIFTY option leg. Strict automatic paper fills therefore remain blocked. A recently received packet, a provider tick time, and a last-trade time cannot substitute for that book-update time.

| Required evidence | Current Upstox path | DhanHQ v2 public documentation | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Exact NIFTY option identity and expiry | Existing instrument/expiry binding and fetched chain in TradePilot | Option chain takes underlying security ID, segment and expiry; expiry-list endpoint exists | Verify contract-master identity and lot size independently before any adapter. |
| Option volume, OI, IV/Greeks and best bid/ask | Captured where present with provider/field scope and missing markers | [Option Chain](https://dhanhq.co/docs/v2/option-chain/) describes all of these; one unique chain request per three seconds | Useful observation fields, not a fill-quality certification. |
| Depth and order counts | V3 quantities exist; current fill snapshot does not contain unique buyer/seller counts | [Live Market Feed](https://dhanhq.co/docs/v2/live-market-feed/) full packet includes five bid/ask levels, quantities and numbers of orders; [Full Market Depth](https://dhanhq.co/docs/v2/full-market-depth/) offers 20/200 levels for NSE equity and derivatives | Orders are not unique buyers/sellers. Do not label or model them as people. |
| Per-leg executable-book update time | [V3 feed](https://upstox.com/developer/api-documentation/v3/get-market-data-feed/) documents `currentTs` as tick/message time and `ltpc.ltt` as last-trade time; separate book-update provenance remains unknown | Reviewed option-chain, live-feed and full-depth field layouts document last-trade time and depth, but no separate timestamp for each bid/ask book update | Both remain **UNKNOWN** for the strict fill gate. Local receipt/change intervals may be retained as observations only. |
| Source rights, retention and commercial terms | Existing account/provider access does not itself prove research retention or redistribution rights | Dhan [support](https://dhan.co/support/platforms/dhanhq-api/how-to-access-dhan-api/) describes a ₹499 plus tax monthly Data API subscription with recurring debit; research retention/redistribution rights were not established by the reviewed API pages | No purchase, paid probe or historical reuse until rights and cost are reviewed for the exact intended use. |
| Real-session reliability | Prior bounded Upstox V3 probes showed changing quotes/depth during `NORMAL_OPEN`, without proving book time | No authenticated DhanHQ account, NIFTY contract mapping, normal-session packet or latency observation has been tested here | Treat DhanHQ capability as documented, not accepted runtime evidence. |

Before reconsidering a provider, obtain a written field definition and sample demonstrating whether **each option-leg bid/ask** has an exchange-origin book-update timestamp, how corrections/reconnects are represented, and whether NIFTY option data may be retained for the frozen paper study. Then run a bounded read-only `NORMAL_OPEN` capture with instrument, exchange session, provider event time, server receipt time, bid/ask/size, sequence/gap and an independent clock reference. Reconcile the sample against the declared license and costs. A negative or incomplete response keeps `NO_TRADE` and strict fills blocked; it does not authorize an indicative fill or a replacement timestamp.

This decision changes no provider configuration, account data, paper positions, fills or live-order setting.
