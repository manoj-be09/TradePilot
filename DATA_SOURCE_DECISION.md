# NIFTY paper-data source decision — 21 October 2026

**Decision: retain Upstox as the configured observation provider; do not add DhanHQ or Kite execution or buy a data plan now.** Upstox and DhanHQ's reviewed public documentation does not prove a distinct exchange-origin update time for the executable bid/ask on each NIFTY option leg. Kite is a stronger technical candidate, but its data-use permission for this paper-trading study is unresolved. Strict automatic paper fills therefore remain blocked. A recently received packet, a provider tick time, and a last-trade time cannot substitute for qualified book-update evidence.

A separate [NSE F&O private-use answer](https://community.upstox.com/t/analytics-token-feed-v3-and-personal-nse-f-o-data-use-confirmation/18203) says personal live display and derived analysis via Feed V3 need no separate exchange-data licence or API/market-data charge for the described use. A third [storage answer](https://community.upstox.com/t/clarification-on-storing-upstox-nse-market-data-locally-for-private-research-backtesting/18112) explicitly includes raw depth, long-term private research and replay, but its question concerns NSE cash equities, so it is supporting context rather than NIFTY F&O-specific scope.

For the owner's private, single-user NIFTY paper and research study, an additional individual permission request does not appear necessary for the above **Feed V3** private retention/replay use. This is a review of published community guidance, not an account-specific legal approval, a public redistribution right, or evidence that every REST chain/bar endpoint has identical retention terms. Keep raw provider data out of the public Git repository and away from third-party access. The repository already tracks the official V3 proto and generated bindings. Upstox's [public SDK example](https://github.com/upstox/upstox-python/blob/master/examples/websocket/market_data/v3/MarketDataFeedV3.proto) publishes the same schema under the SDK's [MIT licence](https://github.com/upstox/upstox-python/blob/master/LICENSE.txt); [the required notice](third_party/upstox/LICENSE.txt) now accompanies the copy. This software licence is separate from market-data redistribution rights. Review that separate issue, the actual endpoint, storage path, availability time and captured raw artifacts before checking Day 6 complete. The earlier UNKNOWN rights statements below record what was known at their dates and are superseded **only for the scoped private Feed V3 use** by this section.

The [Upstox bid/ask freshness answer](https://community.upstox.com/t/v3-full-feed-how-to-verify-bid-ask-depth-freshness/18041) separately confirms that currentTs is Upstox message-generation time, depth can be carried forward, and V3 has no separate exchange-origin depth timestamp or sequence. Thus private retention permission does **not** unblock strict executable bid/ask timing or genuine paper fills. Continue observation, explicit NO_TRADE and the normal-session probe without fabricating book time.

## Observation-time contract — 23 October 2026

Treat these as distinct fields for every future quote review: `last_trade_time`
is the reported transaction time; `timestamp` plus `timestamp_basis` is the
provider response/tick time when supplied; `book_as_of` plus
`book_timestamp_basis` is the bid/ask update time only when the provider proves
that meaning; `received_at` is the TradePilot process time after receiving a
packet or HTTP response; and the feed journal's `observed_at` is when that
observation was written. Clock offset, reconnects and delayed delivery must be
reported separately. The same numeric time in two fields does not make their
provenance equivalent.

The current Upstox adapter sets `book_as_of=None` and
`book_timestamp_basis=unknown`; a fresh `received_at` or changing bid/ask
cannot turn it into exchange-book evidence. Quote reception is now retained
separately from the provider tick/response timestamp in future Upstox quotes
and bounded feed-journal rows. Existing saved positions are untouched. This is
an observation contract and software check, not approval for strict F&O fills
or retention rights beyond the existing bounded diagnostics.

| Time or missing state | Accepted interpretation | Paper-fill decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| REST chain response received by TradePilot | Local receipt time, not a provider or exchange event | Useful for observation ordering; cannot certify book age. |
| WebSocket `currentTs` when supplied | Provider tick/message time; `ltpc.ltt` remains last-trade time | Neither substitutes for a leg's bid/ask update time. |
| WebSocket message without `currentTs` | Local `received_at` only; event-time basis is `unknown` | Fail closed. The stream receipt is not labeled an API response or exchange event. |
| Per-leg `book_as_of` missing or basis unknown | Executable bid/ask update time unavailable | Strict fill blocked even if quote and receipt appear recent. |
| Exchange-labeled book time stale/future | Clock or delivery inconsistency until resolved | Strict fill blocked; never replace it with local receipt time. |
| Disconnect, reconnect, gap or clock offset | Observation continuity/latency uncertain | Expire cached quote and measure separately; no invented exchange timestamp. |

The deterministic timestamp cases in `test_market_data_quality.py`, `test_upstox_market_data.py` and `test_upstox_feed_probe.py` cover missing book time, stale/future chain/quote/book times, changed-book observations, clock-offset calculation and WebSocket missing-tick labeling. **Day 4 contract/fixture package: PASS for software semantics; exchange-origin timing proof remains BLOCKED.**

## Kite Connect review — 21 October 2026

Kite's [full-mode WebSocket packet](https://kite.trade/docs/connect/v3/websocket/) carries five bid and five ask levels, per-level quantity and order count, daily volume, total pending buy/sell quantities, open interest, last-trade time and an **exchange timestamp** in the same instrument packet. Zerodha [explains](https://kite.trade/forum/discussion/3158/difference-between-last-traded-timestamp-and-exchange-timestamp) that the exchange timestamp is the tick time and advances on depth changes without a trade. The [instrument master](https://kite.trade/docs/connect/v3/market-data-and-instruments/) includes NFO option expiry, strike, type, lot size and tick size. This makes a full-mode NIFTY option stream worth testing as a **packet-level book-time candidate**. It is not tick-by-tick exchange coverage; Zerodha says streamed ticks can be coalesced. Second-resolution timestamps also do not establish order within one second. Counts of orders or total pending quantities are not unique buyers and sellers.

The current [Kite pricing page](https://support.zerodha.com/category/trading-and-markets/general-kite/kite-api/articles/what-are-the-charges-for-kite-apis) says the free Personal tier excludes live and historical market data; Connect with WebSocket and historical candles costs ₹500 per app per month. A [Zerodha staff response](https://kite.trade/forum/discussion/16304/written-clarification-for-self-built-kite-connect-trading-system) says permanent data storage and paper trading with Kite live data are restricted. The response does not define a permission pathway for this exact private TradePilot use. Do **not** subscribe, authenticate, capture, persist, display or use Kite data for paper fills before obtaining written permission covering the study's live paper simulation, retention, research and UI use. If permitted, a bounded read-only `NORMAL_OPEN` capture must verify per-option token/expiry mapping, full-mode depth and timestamp behavior, receive latency, gaps/reconnects and sequence ambiguity before the fill gate can be reconsidered. Neither documentation nor a synthetic fixture passes runtime acceptance.

## Day 5 decision — 31 October 2026

**Decision: no selected NIFTY option leg has a usable exchange-origin executable-book time on Upstox Feed V3. Status: `NOT USABLE`, so the strict book-time gate stays BLOCKED.**

Evidence is two hours of the privately recorded 30 October normal session: about 48,000 frames, 2.4 million ticks and 495 instruments. Raw frames stay private.

- The feed schema's only time fields are `LTPC.ltt` (last trade), `OHLC.ts` (bar), `FeedResponse.currentTs` (one per message) and `StatusInfo.updatedTime` (market status). None dates a leg's bid/ask.
- About 87% of top-of-book changes arrived with no change in last-trade time, so `ltt` cannot stand in for book time.
- `currentTs` was at or ahead of local receipt in most sampled frames (median about 200 ms), so it needs clock-offset care, and it is message time in any case.

This covers one session and one provider. Depth changing in frames shows the book is live; it does not date any update. Paper fills on this feed stay indicative under the local educational setting.
